About us.

Who we are

A church family
in Ipswich.

Freedom Church is a Christ-centred, Spirit-led church community in Ipswich, devoted to seeing people encounter God, grow as disciples, and live out the gospel in everyday life.

We believe Jesus is alive and active today and that every person is invited into a transforming relationship with Him. We are a community built on grace, faith and authentic relationships, seeking to see lives renewed by the power of God.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a deeper walk with Jesus, there is a place for you here. Come as you are and journey with us.

Our vision

Behold. Belong.
Become. Bring.

Four words that shape everything we do. Tap each one to read more.

Behold God.

Encountering God. Discovering His presence.

We believe everything begins with Jesus. We're passionate about creating space for people to encounter God through worship, prayer, and His Word. Whether you've followed Jesus for years or are exploring faith for the very first time, our heart is that everyone would have the opportunity to behold who God is and experience His love for themselves.

Belong to His church.

Real people. Genuine relationships. A place to belong.

We believe life is better together. Church isn't about having it all together; it's about being part of a community where you can be yourself, build authentic friendships, and experience genuine love and care. At Freedom, we want everyone to feel welcomed, known, valued, and part of the family.

Become a disciple.

Be with Jesus. Become like Jesus. Do what Jesus did.

Following Jesus is more than something we believe; it's a way of life. We want to grow as everyday disciples who spend time with Jesus, are transformed to become more like Him, and learn to live as He lived. It's a lifelong journey of faith, growth, and discovering what it means to follow Jesus in every part of our lives.

Bring change.

Serving others. Living generously. Sharing hope.

We believe following Jesus makes a difference, not just in our own lives, but in the world around us. Through serving, generosity, and sharing the hope of Jesus, we want to make a meaningful impact both within our church and beyond its walls. Everyone has something to contribute, and together we can bring God's love into our homes, workplaces, neighbourhoods, and communities.

Meet our pastors

Lachlan &
Kristie Mackay

Lead Pastors

Lachlan and Kristie have been part of the Freedom family since the church was first planted in 2022. They love people, love the local church, and are passionate about helping others discover who Jesus is and the difference He can make in their lives.

Alongside raising their three children, they have a heart for seeing people of all ages and backgrounds find a place to belong, build genuine friendships, and grow in their faith.

Lachlan is passionate about teaching the Bible, helping people explore the big questions of faith, and equipping others to live out their faith in everyday life. Alongside pastoring at Freedom, he also lectures in theology and ministry at Christian Heritage College. Kristie brings a warm, creative, and caring heart to the team, with a particular passion for children and families.

Together, their hope is that Freedom would be a church where you can come as you are, feel right at home, and discover what it means to follow Jesus.

Whether you've grown up in church, haven't been for years, or are just curious about faith, Lachlan and Kristie would love to meet you!

Our beliefs

What we
believe

The truths that shape who we are and how we live. Tap any belief to read more.

01 The Eternal Godhead

We believe in God, who is eternal and self-existent, revealed as one being in three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and the Creator of all things.

02 The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, who was conceived of the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived a sinless life, ministering in teaching, healing, and miracles. He was crucified and died on our behalf. He rose from the dead and ascended into heaven, where He is now seated and exalted at the right hand of the Father as the risen Lord and Intercessor.

03 The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit, fully divine, who proceeds from the Father and the Son. He convicts, regenerates and sanctifies, and guides believers into all truth.

04 The Holy Bible

We believe that the Bible is the inerrant, inspired and infallible Word of God and our highest authority for faith and practice.

05 Creation

We believe that God created the universe and that all people are created in the image of God, to love and worship God.

06 The Devil

We believe in the existence and person of the devil, who actively opposes the purposes of God.

07 The Fall of Humanity

We believe that all people have rejected God through their sinful acts and are separated from God.

08 The Atonement

We believe that salvation is found in Jesus Christ alone who, by His suffering, shed blood, death on the cross, and resurrection, has made full atonement for the sins of all people. We receive salvation by grace through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ.

09 The Church

We believe that the Church, the body of Christ, is the community of people who confess faith in Jesus Christ. While universal, the Church is local in expression and witness. It exists for worship, fellowship, evangelism, discipleship, and mission. Gifts of ministry are given by Christ to strengthen and mature the Church.

10 Water Baptism

We believe in water baptism by single immersion in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. It is a public declaration of a person’s faith in, and identification with, the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

11 The Lord’s Supper

We believe in the Lord’s Supper, which is in symbolic remembrance of the sacrifice of Jesus Christ until He returns.

12 Sanctification

We believe in the regenerating work of the Holy Spirit, who convicts people of their sin and inspires repentance and faith. The Holy Spirit transforms believers into the likeness of Jesus Christ.

13 The Baptism in the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, which is a transformative experience, distinct from and subsequent to salvation. It is available to all believers and accompanied by the normative experience of speaking in tongues. It results in empowerment for effective witness to the world.

14 Gifts of the Holy Spirit

We believe that the Holy Spirit imparts spiritual gifts to believers for ministry to one another, the Church and the world.

15 Divine Healing

We believe that God heals today through the atoning work of Jesus Christ. By praying in faith for healing and miracles we witness to and continue the ministry of Jesus Christ.

16 The Second Coming of Christ

We believe in, and look forward to, the imminent and personal return of Jesus Christ to gather His people to Himself, and to judge the living and the dead. His kingdom will have no end.
