Your place
to belong.

Freedom Youth is for everyone in Grade 7 to 12. It's a place to make real friends, ask big questions and discover who Jesus is for yourself.

We meet every Friday night during school terms, and on Sundays we've got seating saved so you can hang out together in the service.

Three kinds
of Friday

Our program rotates through three different nights, so every week is a little different.

01

Encounter
night

Worship, a message and space to encounter God together. Nights to go deeper and hear from Him.

02

Games
night

Big games, lots of laughs and the perfect night to bring a friend who's never been before.

03

Crew
night

Our connect groups. Smaller crews with leaders who care, where you can talk about life and faith and build real friendships.
